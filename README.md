# 🌱 EcoCampus

EcoCampus is a web-based project developed as part of our AAM (Activity/Academic Module) project. The project focuses on promoting environmental awareness and encouraging sustainable practices within the campus community.

---

## 📌 About the Project

EcoCampus is designed to provide students with an interactive and informative platform focused on sustainability and environmentally responsible practices.

The project combines a simple, user-friendly website with educational content to encourage students to become more aware of environmental issues and contribute towards a cleaner and greener campus.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of EcoCampus are:

- 🌱 Promote environmental awareness among students.
- ♻️ Encourage responsible waste management and recycling.
- 🌍 Promote sustainable practices within the campus.
- 💡 Provide students with useful information about sustainability.
- 🤝 Encourage participation in environmental initiatives.
- 💻 Use technology to support environmental awareness.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌱 Interactive and user-friendly interface.
- ♻️ Sustainability and environmental information.
- 🌍 Campus-focused sustainability content.
- 💡 Interactive elements implemented using JavaScript.
- 📱 Responsive web design.
- 🎨 Visually engaging interface.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

The website was developed using:

- **HTML5** – Structure and content of the website.
- **CSS3** – Styling, layout, animations, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** – Interactivity and dynamic functionality.
