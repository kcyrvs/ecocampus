About EcoCampus

How It Works

EcoCampus is a classroom demonstration of a college e-waste reward system. Students log in with a College UID, record items they hand over, and earn Activity Points for responsible disposal.

Why proper e-waste disposal matters

Phones, laptops, batteries, and cables contain metals and chemicals that do not belong in ordinary trash. Informal dumping can leak toxins into soil and water, while recycling recovers valuable materials and keeps hazardous parts out of campus bins.

A simple reward loop: hand over, verify, earn points. It helps students build the habit of using official collection points instead of discarding electronics with mixed waste.

System workflow

  1. Sign in with your College UID.
  2. Select the e-waste type and quantity.
  3. Hand the item over at the campus collection point.
  4. The prototype records a Verified submission and Activity Points.
  5. Review totals and history on your dashboard.

A live campus version would add staff verification before points become final.
